About iEread

Smarter meter reading, less manual work.

iEread is an AI-powered meter reading solution developed by iEngineering Australia to simplify the way meter information is captured, processed and transferred into maintenance systems.

Instead of manually entering meter readings, users can upload meter images, PDFs and supported documents. iEread extracts the important information, matches it with the relevant asset and Reading Point, validates the data and prepares the reading for CARL.

iEread helps transform traditional meter-reading tasks into a faster and more structured digital process.
